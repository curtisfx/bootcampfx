How to Play
  • Breaths. Every stone needs an open neighbor point. Connected stones share breaths. Zero breaths = captured.
  • Ko. You may not play a move that recreates the previous board. Wait one turn.
  • Suicide. You may not play where your own group would have no breath — unless it captures.
  • Ending. Two passes in a row end the game. Score = your ground + your captures. White gets 6.5 komi.
