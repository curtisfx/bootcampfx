I talk them out of it because of what 20 years of coaching in Fullerton taught me:

The math of personal training is rigged against you.

The training worked.

The math didn't.

Your body doesn't change based on how much you pay per session.

It changes based on how many sessions you show up to.

Twice a week maintains.

Four times a week transforms.

That's the whole secret. Every client I've ever watched completely rebuild their body had it working for them. Not genetics. Not a miracle program. Showing up often enough.
